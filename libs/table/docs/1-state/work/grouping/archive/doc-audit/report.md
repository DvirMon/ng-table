## Resolution — 2026-09-19

All 15 `stale` rows and the 1 `drift` row applied to `0-product/grouping.md`. Three findings the
audit did not reach, found while fixing:

- **4.1 moved ❌ → 🟡.** `grouping-static/` now wires a table-wide `when` (group admission,
  ADR-0018) with a `keepBlankRegionsFlat` toggle, AND-combined with a per-column row-count
  threshold on `category`. Blank-region rows stay flat rather than forming three unlabelled
  groups. Mechanism ships; the library's own default is still undecided.
- **4.2's mechanism is `extractValue`/`label`, not `accessor`.** `fixtures/schema.ts` states it
  outright: "Grouping no longer reads a column's `accessor` at all (D7)". The shared fixture
  declares no `extractValue`, so grouping by Owner today merges every row into one
  `object:[object Object]` bucket — the failure the doc claimed was covered.
- **Tally is 10 ✅ / 6 🟡 / 1 ❌**, not the 10/5/2 the audit derived, because of 4.1.

`1 unverified` (F-G1's emptied group) left as-is — it needs a story run, not a doc edit.
