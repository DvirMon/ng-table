Selection under an active filter. Select a few rows, then filter them out of view: they stay selected, and clearing the filter brings the selection back exactly as it was. The two select-all buttons are separate on purpose — one scopes to what you can see, the other to the whole dataset. Sorting never changes the selection; deleting a row does, even one that is filtered out.
